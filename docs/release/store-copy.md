## 0. 文案策略

**一句话定位**：把"弹幕射击很难上手"这个品类痛点，用一个操控方案直接消掉。

三条支撑逻辑，所有文案围绕它们展开：

1. **上手成本 = 0**：不用学操作。手指在触摸板/鼠标上移动到哪，战机就在哪。自动开火。
2. **启动成本 = 0**：网页打开就玩，不下载、不注册、不登录、不联网。
3. **内容不缩水**：4 关战役、4 个 Boss、8 种敌机、皮肤自由设计、连击倍率——不是玩具级 Demo。

**写作红线**：
- ❌ 不用"史诗""震撼""极致"这类空词
- ❌ 不承诺未做的内容（无尽模式、每日挑战、成就、联机全球榜均**未实现**，不得出现在文案里）
- ✅ 每条特性都能在游戏里被立刻验证

## 特性要点（Feature bullets）

- 🎯 **指哪打哪的绝对定位操控** —— 触摸板或鼠标的位置直接就是战机的位置，没有加速度、没有惯性、不用"找回"光标。自动开火，你只管走位。
- 🚀 **4 关战役 · 4 个关底 Boss** —— 近地轨道 → 陨石带 → 电离层 → 深空要塞，每关独立配色与难度曲线，Boss 多阶段弹幕轮换。
- 👾 **8 种敌机机型** —— 直冲、蛇行、射击、精英、回旋、俯冲、狙击、分裂，随战役推进逐步登场。
- 🔥 **火力 5 级变形 + 连击倍率** —— 吃道具升级火力，战机外形随之变形；连续击杀最高叠到 4 倍分数，受击清零。
- 🎨 **皮肤自由设计（内置 Mod）** —— 6 款预设涂装，或自己调机身 / 描边 / 尾焰 / 机翼 / 弹道 + 4 种图案，实时预览，本地保存。
- 🎵 **零音频文件的程序化音效与自适应 BGM** —— 所有声音由代码实时合成，包体极小。
- 💣 **紧急清屏炸弹 · 护盾 · 生命补给** —— 撑不住的时候点一下屏幕，全屏清空。
- 📱 **一份代码，三端可玩** —— 网页直玩、Windows 免安装 EXE、Android APK；网页版支持安装为 PWA 后离线游玩。
- 🔒 **全程离线，零数据采集** —— 不联网、不要账号、不收任何数据，进度全存在你自己设备上。

## Title

**Fighter Shooter · 打飞机**

## Tagline (≤ 40 chars recommended)

> **Your ship goes where your cursor goes. A bullet-hell with zero learning curve.**

Alternatives:

- No controls to learn. Move your finger — your ship is already there.
- Point-and-fly bullet hell. Opens in a browser, no download, no account.

## Feature bullets

- 🎯 **Absolute-position controls** — Your trackpad or mouse position *is* your ship's position. No acceleration, no inertia, no hunting for the cursor. Firing is automatic; all you do is dodge.
- 🚀 **4-stage campaign, 4 bosses** — Low Orbit → Asteroid Belt → Ionosphere → Deep Space Fortress. Each stage has its own palette and difficulty curve, each ends in a multi-phase boss.
- 👾 **8 enemy types** — Straight, weaver, shooter, elite, spinner, diver, sniper and splitter, unlocking as the campaign progresses.
- 🔥 **5 power tiers + combo multiplier** — Your ship physically reshapes as firepower levels up. Chain kills to stack up to a 4× score multiplier — one hit and it's gone.
- 🎨 **Build your own ship skin** — 6 presets, or design your own: hull, trim, engine flame, wings, bullet colour, plus 4 pattern styles. Live preview, saved locally.
- 🎵 **Zero audio files** — Every sound effect and the adaptive soundtrack are synthesised in real time with Web Audio.
- 💣 **Screen-clearing bombs, shields, extra lives** — When it gets ugly, one click wipes the screen.
- 📱 **One codebase, three platforms** — Play in the browser, grab the portable Windows EXE, or the Android APK. The web build installs as a PWA and runs offline.
- 🔒 **Fully offline, zero telemetry** — No network calls, no account, no data collection. Everything stays on your device.

## Short description (≤ 100 words)

> A vertical bullet-hell shooter with exactly one control rule: wherever your mouse or trackpad is, that's where your ship is. Firing is automatic. Four stages, four bosses, eight enemy types, and a ship-skin editor. Runs in your browser — no download, no account, no network.

## Long description

```
Bullet-hell shooters look great. Most people quit at step one: learning the controls.

Fighter Shooter deletes that step entirely.

Wherever your mouse or trackpad is, that's where your ship is. Not "hold a direction key",
not "push a stick and accelerate" — a one-to-one mapping of position to position.
Your finger moves, the ship moves. Firing is automatic.
New players are flying within three seconds. Experienced players discover that this scheme
is brutally precise once the screen fills with bullets.

CAMPAIGN
Four connected stages: Low Orbit → Asteroid Belt → Ionosphere → Deep Space Fortress.
Each has its own colour theme, enemy mix and difficulty curve, and each is guarded by a boss:
Sentinel Mech, Mining Scorpion, Thundercloud Mothership, Annihilation Core.
Bosses run multi-phase health bars and rotate through spread fire, twin spirals and
player-tracking bursts — and they get meaner the lower their health drops.

ENEMIES
Eight types, introduced gradually: straight runners, weavers, shooters and elites early on;
spinners, divers, snipers and splitters later. Kill a splitter and it spits out
three more drones on the way down.

PROGRESSION
Kills drop pickups: Power (P), Shield (S), Bomb (B) and the rare Life (L).
Five firepower tiers, and your ship visibly reshapes at every one of them —
from a single shot up to a five-way spread.
Kill within three seconds of your last kill and the combo builds, up to a 4× score multiplier.
Take a single hit and it resets to zero. Greed or safety — your call.

SHIP SKINS
Six built-in presets: Cyan Knight, Crimson Falcon, Emerald Peregrine, Violet Lightning,
Night Ghost, Golden Wing.
Not your taste? Build one. Hull, trim, engine flame, wings and bullet colour,
plus plain / stripe / hex / carbon patterns. Live preview, saved locally, swap any time.

UNDER THE HOOD
The whole game is plain HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript. No dependencies, no build step.
All art is procedurally drawn vector graphics; all sound effects and music are synthesised
live with Web Audio. Not a single image file, not a single audio file.
That's why it loads instantly and runs completely offline.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY
- Web: open and play; installable as a PWA for offline sessions
- Windows: single-file portable EXE, no installer
- Android: APK
Saves, high scores and skins live on your device. The game never goes online,
never asks for an account, and collects nothing.

CONTROLS
Move: mouse / trackpad / touch drag (WASD and arrow keys also work)
Fire: automatic
Bomb: click the screen (mobile: 💣 bottom-right)
Pause: ESC (mobile: ⏸ top-right)

Available in English and Simplified Chinese. Free to play — tips welcome.
```
